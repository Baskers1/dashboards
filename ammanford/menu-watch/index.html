How this is put together

Places come from the Food Standards Agency's list of food businesses in Carmarthenshire, Swansea, Neath Port Talbot and Powys, narrowed to pubs, restaurants and cafés you can sit down in, plus a few newer places found locally. Canteens, members' clubs and takeaway-only shops are left out.

Every Monday each venue's own website is checked for menus. A menu counts as new when its link was not there the Monday before. This first edition was put together on 8 October 2026, so counting starts on Monday 12 October. Until then the top section shows Christmas and festive menus already published. Menus shown as pictures or PDFs are linked but not read, so check dates and dishes with the venue. Places marked "check" had no recent sign of trading online, and some outlying places (around Brynamman, Llandybie, Gorslas, Cross Hands, Pontyberem and east Llanelli) only have their hygiene rating so far; their websites and reviews are added on the weekly refresh.

Hygiene is the Food Standards Agency rating from 0 to 5, where 5 is best, read on 8 October 2026. It reflects the last inspection, which may be some time ago. TripAdvisor scores are out of 5, with the number of reviews in brackets; a score from only a few reviews means less. OpenTable shows the place takes online bookings there.
