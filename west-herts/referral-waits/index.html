What the numbers mean. Median wait is how long patients currently on the list have waited so far. It is not the time a new referral will wait. The 18-week figure is the share of everyone on the list who has waited 18 weeks or less since referral. Care type groups are my own grouping of NHS treatment specialties.

Site level. NHS England publishes these figures per trust, not per hospital site, day surgery unit or town. West Hertfordshire's figures cover Watford General, Hemel Hempstead and St Albans City together. The area note above gives approximate distances only.

Source. NHS England Referral to Treatment (RTT) incomplete pathways, data through July 2026, and A&E for August 2026, as compiled by HospitalWaits. Cross-check against the NHS England RTT release before relying on it.
