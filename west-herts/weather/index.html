River Levels

About this data

Readings every 15 minutes from the Environment Agency's flood monitoring service (Open Government Licence v3.0, beta service). Each station reports on its own scale in metres, so levels cannot be compared between stations. The shaded band on each chart is the Environment Agency's typical range for that station; the line is the last 24 hours. The Gade, Chess and Colne rise and fall quickly after heavy rain. This is a level reading, not a flood forecast. Check GOV.UK Check for flooding for official warnings.

Flood Warnings

About this data

Flood alerts and warnings published by the Environment Agency for places within 12 km of Croxley Green and Rickmansworth, where the Colne, Gade and Chess flow. Alerts and warnings change quickly, so check GOV.UK Check for flooding for the current position.

Live Sewage Alerts

About this data

Thames Water publishes near-real-time data from the event duration monitors on its storm overflows. This section lists the monitored overflows that discharge to the Colne, Colne Brook, Chess and Misbourne around Croxley Green and Rickmansworth.

Thames Water live mapStream Water Data hubAbout the monitors
  • Discharging: the monitor reports that the overflow is operating now.
  • Finished: the monitor reported a discharge that has stopped. The list shows when it ended and how long it ran.
  • Offline: the monitor is not reporting, so its status is unknown.

Thames Water says its monitors are sensitive and can be set off by things such as a weed moving in front of the sensor, so a reading indicates a discharge rather than confirming one. The data does not measure water quality or volume, and a record here is not a swim-safety rating.
