    Useful websites

      About this page. Who is doing what across Watford, Hemel Hempstead, Rickmansworth, Croxley Green and Sarratt: social enterprises, volunteering and community groups, plus the council and government plans that affect them, local people and wildlife. Deadlines are flagged as they get close.

      How to read this. Days left are counted from today's date in London. "Closes" is the last day to reply, apply or sign up. "Happens" is the day of an event. Red means 7 days or fewer, amber 21 days or fewer. A group marked "Check before you go" was found in a search result or an older article, so confirm it is still running before you travel or hand over money.

      What is not here. Parish council and residents' association notices were read only where a page turned up in a search. Sarratt and Croxley Green have far fewer dated items than Watford or Hemel Hempstead, so an empty result for them does not mean nothing is happening. Some group sites blocked automated reading, and small groups that only use Facebook or word of mouth will be missing. Dates for a few events are derived from a stated rule (for example "third Saturday") and are labelled as such.
