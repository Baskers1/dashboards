    How to read this. A filled dot beside a group or topic means a source names it. A hollow dot is my reading of a likely effect, so treat it as a prompt to check, not a finding.

    Carnoustie, Monifieth and Broughty Ferry. I found no consultation aimed at Carnoustie or Monifieth alone on 26 October 2026. Angus-wide ones, such as the new 2027/28 budget survey, the additional support needs options, the wheelchair accessible taxis survey, outdoor play, the Local Development Plan evidence chapters, the direct payment care rates surveys and the rent review, apply to both and are shown under Carnoustie. The Montrose and Forfar items (the closed golf links lease, the closed Inch Cape wind farm fund survey and the closed Dundee Road 20mph survey) are shown as Angus comparisons, and the 2024 Carnoustie Golf Links consultation is listed as a place to watch. Broughty Ferry items appear under Dundee; I found none open aimed at Broughty Ferry alone, though the Engage Dundee survey has drop-in sessions at Broughty Ferry Library. NHS Tayside's community pharmacy survey and Bield's rent survey apply across the area and show under both places. A Scottish Fire and Rescue Service consultation on the future of Balmossie fire station, which serves Broughty Ferry and Monifieth, ran in 2025, and local posts from mid-2026 report the SFRS Board's decision; it is not listed as a card because it closed well over four months ago.

    Sources checked on 26 October 2026. Dundee City Council consultations (the same four open: electric vehicle chargers, now shown as running to 1 January 2027, the visitor levy, Engage Dundee and Camperdown Park), Engage Angus (one newly listed: the 2027/28 budget consultation phase 1, 19 October to 16 November; the Forfar 20mph survey is now archived; the four care rates pages still show survey links with no closing date), the Scottish Government consultation hub (13 open; the student tenancies consultation closed on 22 October; two new land reform consultations on community engagement and large land holdings, closing 26 January 2027, are aimed mainly at owners of large rural estates and the communities around them, so they are not listed as cards; the rest, such as pelagic fisheries plans, a Social Security Scotland Gaelic plan, road works register fees and agricultural training, are on the hub), NatureScot consultations, the SEPA consultation hub (open items are permit applications, none in Dundee or Angus), the NHS Tayside strategy notice from PKAVS (still closing 12 noon on 31 October), Dundee Health and Social Care Partnership's pages (nothing current), the Inch Cape community fund page, and searches of local news, community council sites and charity notices. I found no new charity or third-sector consultation open to the public for Dundee or Carnoustie; the Alzheimer Scotland survey on dementia support in Dundee that keeps turning up in searches closed in October 2025.
