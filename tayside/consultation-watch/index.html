    How to read this. A filled dot beside a group or topic means a source names it. A hollow dot is my reading of a likely effect, so treat it as a prompt to check, not a finding.

    Carnoustie and Monifieth. I found no consultation aimed at Carnoustie or Monifieth alone on 16 October 2026. Angus-wide ones, such as the additional support needs options, outdoor play, the Local Development Plan evidence chapters, the direct payment care rates surveys and the rent review, apply to both and are shown under Carnoustie. The Montrose items (the closed golf links lease and the Inch Cape wind farm fund) are shown as Angus comparisons, and the 2024 Carnoustie Golf Links consultation is listed as a place to watch. Broughty Ferry items appear under Dundee; I found none open aimed at Broughty Ferry alone. A Scottish Fire and Rescue Service consultation on the future of Balmossie fire station, which serves Broughty Ferry and Monifieth, ran in 2025, and local posts from mid-2026 report the SFRS Board's decision; it is not listed as a card because it closed well over four months ago.

    Sources checked on 16 October 2026. Dundee City Council consultations (four open: electric vehicle chargers, the visitor levy, Engage Dundee and Camperdown Park), the council's planning consultations page (none active), Engage Angus and each project page I could open, Angus Council news, the Scottish Government consultation hub (13 open, the same list as on 15 October, with the Passivhaus-equivalent standard closing today; this page lists those most relevant, and the rest, such as pelagic fisheries plans, a Social Security Scotland Gaelic plan, road works register fees and agricultural training, are on the hub), the Scottish Government's council reform proposal, NatureScot consultations (the biodiversity metric is the only one listed as open with a current date), NHS Tayside's strategy notice published by PKAVS, and searches of local news and charity notices, which found the Inch Cape community fund survey run with SKS Scotland CIC. I found no other charity or third-sector consultation open to the public for Dundee or Carnoustie; Bield, a housing charity with homes in Angus, lists tenant-only consultations on its own site. Engage Angus also has a Letham flood experiences survey (closes 23 October 2026) and a tenant value-for-money satisfaction survey with no closing date shown; neither is listed here because one is for Letham only and the other gives no dates. It also lists a Brechin River Street regeneration project, whose page I could not open.
