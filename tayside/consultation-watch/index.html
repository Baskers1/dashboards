    How to read this. A filled dot beside a group or topic means a source names it. A hollow dot is my reading of a likely effect, so treat it as a prompt to check, not a finding.

    Carnoustie, Monifieth and Broughty Ferry. I found no consultation aimed at Carnoustie or Monifieth alone on 20 October 2026. Angus-wide ones, such as the additional support needs options, the wheelchair accessible taxis survey, outdoor play, the Local Development Plan evidence chapters, the direct payment care rates surveys and the rent review, apply to both and are shown under Carnoustie. The Montrose items (the closed golf links lease and the Inch Cape wind farm fund) are shown as Angus comparisons, and the 2024 Carnoustie Golf Links consultation is listed as a place to watch. Broughty Ferry items appear under Dundee; I found none open aimed at Broughty Ferry alone, though the Engage Dundee survey has drop-in sessions at Broughty Ferry Library. NHS Tayside's community pharmacy survey and Bield's rent survey apply across the area and show under both places. A Scottish Fire and Rescue Service consultation on the future of Balmossie fire station, which serves Broughty Ferry and Monifieth, ran in 2025, and local posts from mid-2026 report the SFRS Board's decision; it is not listed as a card because it closed well over four months ago.

    Sources checked on 20 October 2026. Dundee City Council consultations (the same four open: electric vehicle chargers, the visitor levy, Engage Dundee and Camperdown Park; no new consultation in the council's October news), Engage Angus (one newly listed: the wheelchair accessible taxis and private hire survey for licensed drivers and operators, closing 5pm on 30 October; the personal assistant rates page now opens but shows no closing date), the Scottish Government consultation hub (the same 12 open, none added since 16 October; this page lists those most relevant, and the rest, such as pelagic fisheries plans, a Social Security Scotland Gaelic plan, road works register fees and agricultural training, are on the hub), NatureScot consultations (the biodiversity metric is the only one with a current date; a National Nature Reserve management plan consultation is also listed, with no dates shown), the NHS Tayside strategy notice from PKAVS, Dundee Health and Social Care Partnership's consultations page (nothing current), the Inch Cape community fund survey run with SKS Scotland CIC, and searches of local news and charity notices. I found no new charity or third-sector consultation open to the public for Dundee or Carnoustie; an Alzheimer Scotland survey on dementia support in Dundee that turned up in searches was from 2025, and a Tayside circular economy survey for businesses gives no dates. Engage Angus also has a Letham flood study and a tenant value-for-money survey with no closing date shown; neither is listed here because one is for Letham only and the other gives no dates.

    Not covered. Planning applications, Transport Scotland roadworks orders, Tay Cities Deal projects, community council surveys, Perth and Kinross Council, Police Scotland, Tactran, the Tay Road Bridge Joint Board, Scottish Water, SEPA, Planning and Environmental Appeals Division cases and Scottish Parliament committee calls were searched on 12 to 20 October 2026 but not checked page by page (the SEPA consultations page and the Scottish Parliament calls for views page would not open on 19 October); searches found no open consultation from them for this area. Two Scottish Parliament committees have a call for views on hyperscale data centres (7 October to 15 November 2026), aimed mainly at experts, which is not listed as a card. Local posts report talks between Stagecoach, Angus Council and community representatives about bus route changes affecting Monifieth; I found no public consultation on them. No Dundee or Angus petitions archive is included.
