    How to read this. A filled dot beside a group or topic means a source names it. A hollow dot is my reading of a likely effect, so treat it as a prompt to check, not a finding.

    Carnoustie and Monifieth. I found no consultation aimed at Carnoustie or Monifieth alone on 13 October 2026. Angus-wide ones, such as the additional support needs options, outdoor play, the Local Development Plan evidence chapters and the rent review, apply to both and are shown under Carnoustie. The Montrose items (the closed golf links lease and the Inch Cape wind farm fund) are shown as Angus comparisons, and the 2024 Carnoustie Golf Links consultation is listed as a place to watch. Broughty Ferry items appear under Dundee; I found none aimed at Broughty Ferry alone.

    Sources checked on 13 October 2026. Dundee City Council consultations (four open: electric vehicle chargers, the visitor levy, Engage Dundee and Camperdown Park), the council's planning consultations page (none active), Engage Angus and each project page, Angus Council news, the Scottish Government consultation hub (13 open; this page lists those most relevant, and the rest, such as pelagic fisheries plans, a Social Security Scotland Gaelic plan, road works register fees and agricultural training, are on the hub), NatureScot consultations (carried over from 12 October), NHS Tayside's strategy notice published by PKAVS, and searches of local news and charity notices, which found the Inch Cape community fund survey run with SKS Scotland CIC. I found no other charity or third-sector consultation open for Dundee or Carnoustie. Engage Angus also has a Letham flood experiences survey (closes 23 October 2026) and a tenant value-for-money satisfaction survey with no closing date shown; neither is listed here because one is for Letham only and the other gives no dates.
