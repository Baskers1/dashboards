      About this page. Who is doing what across Dundee, Broughty Ferry, Monifieth, Carnoustie, Arbroath and Angus, with Perth and the Fife side of the Tay (Newport-on-Tay, Tayport, Leuchars and St Andrews) added on 6 October 2026: social enterprises, volunteering and community groups, plus the council and government plans that affect them, local people and wildlife. Deadlines are flagged as they get close.

      How to read this. Days left are counted from today's date in London. "Closes" is the last day to reply, apply or sign up. "Happens" is the day of an event. Red means 7 days or fewer, amber 21 days or fewer. A group marked "Check before you go" was found in a search result or an older article, so confirm it is still running before you travel or hand over money.

      What is not here. I found no open consultation on Dundee's next Local Development Plan, no live Foundation Scotland fund aimed at Dundee or Angus, and no verified Dundee Botanic Garden or Loch of Kinnordy events, because those sites blocked automated reading. Many small groups, including Carnoustie allotments and several Arbroath groups, only use Facebook, so they are not listed. Monifieth Eco Force, Bonnie Dundee and Broughty Ferry Rotary's litter pickers are listed from the Keep Scotland Beautiful Anchor Groups page. Angus Community Connector and the Friends of the Earth Tayside directory list more. The Perth, Newport-on-Tay, Tayport, Leuchars and St Andrews filters are new, so they show only what I could confirm on 6 October 2026, plus anything that covers every town. Expect fewer entries there than for Dundee for now.
