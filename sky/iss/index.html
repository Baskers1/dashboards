The ISS travels about 28,000 km/h, so even a fresh position is up to 10 minutes behind and the real ISS may be thousands of km along its orbit. Data is saved from Open Notify by a script on a home Mac every 10 minutes; if the Mac sleeps this page says so rather than showing an old position as current.
