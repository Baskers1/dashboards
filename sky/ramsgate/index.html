Met Office outlook (issued 20:59 (GMT) on Sun 18 Oct 2026)
Background space weather conditions are expected until the onset of fast winds from a coronal hole. This is most likely on 20 Oct (UTC), but could be as early as late on 19 Oct. It could then enhance the aurora from 20 Oct, leading to more widespread views across high latitudes. This will also bring the chance for some glimpses of the aurora further south, perhaps into northern Scotland and similar geomagnetic latitudes.

Ramsgate sits far south, so it usually needs a red alert to see aurora by eye. Amber is worth a north-facing phone photo on a clear night. Live alerts: AuroraWatch UK (free app and email alerts) · Met Office space weather
