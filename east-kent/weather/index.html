Air Quality

About this data
Hourly readings from Defra's UK-AIR network (data provisional and unvalidated; Open Government Licence v3.0). The stations are about 54 km away, so this is a regional picture, not Ramsgate itself. Defra lists a Canterbury station but it publishes no readings here. Index uses the UK Daily Air Quality Index: 1-3 Low, 4-6 Moderate, 7-9 High, 10 Very High. This is not health advice. Index scale: 1 (best) to 10.

Tides

Today's Tide

About this data

High (H) and low (L) water for the selected town (Ramsgate, Margate, Broadstairs and Deal each have their own table; Sandwich borrows Ramsgate's), height in metres, times already adjusted for BST. Sunrise and sunset shown for planning daylight. Tables: tidetimes.org.uk (derived in part from UK Hydrographic Office data; personal use). The curve is a smooth estimate drawn between the listed high and low waters, and shaded areas are night. Weather and wind can shift tide times and heights a little.

Bathing Water Quality

    Live Sewage Alerts

    About this data and live map

    Surfers Against Sewage run a near-real-time map of sewage discharge alerts for about 850 UK swim spots. Check it for Ramsgate Sands, Broadstairs and Margate before anyone paddles or swims.

    Open the live sewage mapWhat the alerts meanStorm overflow map (sewagedata.co.uk)
    • No alert: data is coming in and there is no sign of reduced water quality.
    • Sewage alert: a confirmed discharge, which stays active for 48 hours after it stops.
    • Pollution risk forecast: bathing discouraged; bathing season only.
    • Pollution incident: confirmed event such as a burst pipe or oil spill; bathing not advised.
    • Out of season: no bathing-water data outside the season, which ended on 30 September.

    This page can't show Surfers Against Sewage's live data itself: they don't publish a data feed or reuse licence, and the map can't be embedded here. Alert wording is summarised from Surfers Against Sewage's Data HQ page.

    Walk Ideas

    About these walks

    Distances and times are approximate, and the links go to other websites whose routes may differ a little from the ones described here. Bus routes and timetables change: check Stagecoach South East (Loop, 8/8X, 9, 45) before setting off, and take a phone and water. Further-afield walks come from Explore Kent and Kent Ramblers (Kent Ramblers also lists many walks in west Kent, which are too far away to include here). Visit Thanet lists guided walks, such as Ramsgate costumed walks in July and August and Broadstairs history walks, on its walking page. The small tiles beside each walk are simple illustrations of its type (coast or inland), not photographs of the route.
