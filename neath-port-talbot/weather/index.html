River Levels

About this data

Latest readings from Natural Resources Wales river level stations (Open Government Licence v3.0), with NRW's own rising, steady or falling trend. Each station reports on its own scale in metres, so levels cannot be compared between stations. The usual range (shaded band, where known) is the range the level is in 90% of the time, as published by RiverLevels.uk from NRW data. The line builds up from the readings collected each time this page is refreshed. The Neath, Dulais, Clydach and Afan rise quickly after heavy rain on the hills above the valleys. This is a level reading, not a flood forecast. Check NRW flood warnings for official warnings and NRW river levels for full charts.

Flood Warnings

About this data

Flood alerts and warnings published by Natural Resources Wales for the rivers and coast of Neath Port Talbot and the rest of Wales. Alerts and warnings change quickly, so check NRW flood warnings and alerts or call Floodline on 0345 988 1188 for the current position.
