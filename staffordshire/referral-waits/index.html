What the numbers mean. Median wait is how long patients currently on the list have waited so far. It is not the time a new referral will wait. The 18-week figure is the share of everyone on the list who has waited 18 weeks or less since referral. Care type groups are my own grouping of NHS treatment specialties.

Deaths registered

View

Source. ONS, Deaths registered in England and Wales, (released ): Table 5 (cause, age and place of death, national) and Table 6 (place of death by area of usual residence). Years are year of registration, 2016 to 2025, extended back to 2010-2014 using earlier ONS annual reference tables (2015 and 2006-2009 are not available). For 2010-2014, area-of-usual-residence breakdowns are only available at Country/Region level; current NHS Integrated Care Boards and Local Health Boards did not yet exist and are shown as unavailable for those years.

Institution here means type of place, not a named hospital. ONS records where a death occurred (NHS hospitals, care homes, hospices, home and so on). It does not name individual hospitals or trusts and carries no CQC rating, so those cannot be filters on this data. The area is where the person usually lived, not where they died. Percentages are the share of that area's deaths.

Source. NHS England Digital, Summary Hospital-level Mortality Indicator (SHMI), trusts. CQC ratings are from the CQC tab.

Reading it carefully. The SHMI compares deaths in hospital or within 30 days of discharge with the number expected for that trust's patients. It is not a measure of avoidable deaths or of quality on its own, and a higher figure does not show poor care. Observed and expected counts in the file are rounded to the nearest 5.
